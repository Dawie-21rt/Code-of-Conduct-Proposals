## MERIT-BASED CODE OF CONDUCT
We, [Club Name] provide this Code of Conduct to determine what members can and cannot do. To also have safety in our club.
This Code of Conduct ensures members adhere to our ("the Club") standards of:
- Fairness.
- Justice.
- Safety.
- Respect for one another.

### Article 1 - Merits
1. Merits act as marks awarded for positive discipline described in the Codes of Behavior.
2. Merits also earn club members milestones such as leadership positions, etc.

### Article 2 - Demerits
1. Demerits act as negative marks awarded against a club member for an offense/neglect of rules.
2. There are thresholds for what disciplinary actions are taken in the table of offense/neglect of rules.

### Article 3 - Club Rules
1. The best discipline is self-discipline.
2. Be civil and respectful.
3. Harassment is not tolerated.
